Arav Scanner

A simple document scanner for Android. Point the camera at a page and get a clean PDF.

  • Scan documents and ID cards with automatic edge detection and cropping
  • Save scans as PDF or share them as images
  • Read text from scans (English, Hindi and Marathi), on the phone itself
  • Scan QR codes and barcodes
  • Organise scans into folders and search them
  • Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
  • Optional backup of your PDFs to your own Google Drive

Your scans stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
