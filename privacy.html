Arav Scanner privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Summary

Arav Scanner keeps your scans on your phone. The developer does not run any server and does not receive, store or see your scans, your text or your Google account details.

What stays on your phone

  • Scanned pages, the PDFs made from them, their titles, folders and recognised text are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics of its own.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off unless you choose "Link Google Drive". If you do:

  • The app asks Google for one permission only: access to files the app itself creates in your Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • It creates a folder named "Arav Scanner" in your Drive and uploads the PDF of each scan there. Uploads go directly from your phone to Google.
  • The access token Google issues is used on your phone only, to make those uploads. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • You can stop at any time with "Unlink Google Drive" in the app, or by removing Arav Scanner at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Arav Scanner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not shared with anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person.

Google components inside the app

The camera scanner, text recognition and QR/barcode reader are provided by Google Play services (ML Kit). Images are processed on your device. Google may collect technical usage and performance information from these components, as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

App lock

The optional app lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock check. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never receives your biometric data or PIN.

Sharing

A scan leaves the app only when you choose to share it, save it elsewhere, or back it up to Google Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
